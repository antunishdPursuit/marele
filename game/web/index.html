Elena's signals

What happened

    Día de Muertos, after midnight

    You are Tía Elena. Years ago, a repair on the family computer went wrong, and your voice was left inside it.

    Tonight your niece Marycel sits down at that same old computer to save the family video for the ofrenda, the altar for the family's dead. She decides for herself what to do. You can only reach her through the computer.

    The night plays out in three rooms at once: three versions of the same night.

    How to play

    1. Watch

      Marycel works in three rooms at once. Click a small room picture to watch that room.

    2. Send a signal

      You are Elena. Pick a signal from Elena's signals. Every room gets it, but each Marycel notices it only if she can see or hear it.

    3. Answer her

      She finds two voices on the old drive. Before moving one, she asks you: answer Yes, No, Danger or More tests within about 45 seconds.

    4. Mind the Echo

      Something else is in there with you. It can only repeat sounds Marycel has already played out loud.

    Pause stops the night. End the night shows how each room turned out.

    Made for the Hacktoberfest 2026 "Ghost in the Machine" challenge. Marycel is a small open model (Qwen3-8B) fine-tuned for this game, and the game's rules check every step she takes. A cookie keeps your night going and your browser remembers the sound setting; nothing else is stored. Source code on GitHub (MIT)
