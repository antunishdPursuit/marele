What happened

    Día de Muertos, after midnight

    You are Tía Elena. Years ago a recovery on the family computer went wrong, and your voice was left inside it.

    Tonight, in three rooms, three versions of your niece Marycel sit down with that same old machine to save the family video for the ofrenda. She decides for herself what to do next. You can only reach her through the computer: a few letters on the screen, a tone, a printed note, a fragment of the old recording.

    Something else is in there with you. It can only repeat what Marycel has already played.
