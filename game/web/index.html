    Día de Muertos, after midnight

    You are Tía Elena. Years ago, repairing the family computer went wrong, and your voice was left inside it.

    Tonight your niece Marycel sits down at that same old computer to save the family video for the ofrenda (the family altar for the dead). It happens in three rooms at once: three versions of the same night. She decides for herself what to do. You can only reach her through the computer: a few letters on the screen, a tone, a printed note, a piece of the old recording.

    • Watch. Click a small room picture to see that room.
    • She will find two voices on the old drive. Help her tell which one is yours.
    • Before she moves a voice to the new drive, she asks you. Answer with the buttons that appear: Yes, No, Danger or More tests.
    • Something else is in there with you, the Echo. It can only repeat what Marycel has already played out loud.

    About: made for the Hacktoberfest 2026 "Ghost in the Machine" challenge. Marycel is a small open model (Qwen3-8B) fine-tuned for this game; the game's rules check every step she takes. A cookie keeps your night going and your browser remembers the sound setting; nothing else about you is stored.
